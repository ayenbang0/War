**Count:** 33 findings: P0 0, P1 7, P2 17, P3 9.

**What already works and should be kept:** strokes are converted to world space when they are captured (`pen.ts:47-53`). Camera dt is real time and clamped to 0.25 s (`main.ts:313`), and pan speed is `speed*dt/zoom`, so it does not depend on frame rate. The camera has no smoothing, so it cannot overshoot or jitter. `zoomAt` keeps the world point under the cursor (`camera.ts:72-83`), and the clamp limits the *view* rather than the centre, centring on any axis where the map is smaller than the view (`camera.ts:92-99`), as camera-systems asks. `blur` clears held keys (`camera-controls.ts:64-66`). `touch-action: none` and pointer capture are in place (`index.html:7`, `pen.ts:64-68`). The recogniser is pure and tested by a confusion matrix.

## Notes for the kingdom map (PLAN.md §B.5, separate recogniser context)

**Reuse:**
- the pure `recognize(raw, ctx)` shape: world-space capture, priority-ordered discrimination, ties broken on id;
- the corpus and confusion matrix, built *with the battle vocabulary live*;
- `Camera.zoomAt` and the view clamp;
- real-time dt for the camera;
- the ghost-at-pen-tip contract.

**Avoid repeating:**
- silent `null` commits (PC-02);
- previews computed differently from commits (PC-03, PC-07). Make one function that returns `{preview, commitResult}`;
- hidden timed windows (PC-11);
- writing state from the frame loop (PC-05);
- module-level `keydown` listeners (PC-06, PC-22);
- fixed module-constant zoom limits. Parametrise `Camera` with per-layer zoom bounds, derived from the viewport (PC-20);
- tap and grab thresholds that ignore screen size (PC-14). B.5 "tap region opens Region card" makes the tap threshold even more central.

**Needs new work first:**
- an eased `flyTo`/`cut` for layer transitions (PC-24);
- B.5 "merge" (a stroke from army to own army) collides with today's move-to-own-squad reading, and needs corpus pairs against march;
- the ETA label on the march ghost needs the label-size and clamp fixes (PC-17).
